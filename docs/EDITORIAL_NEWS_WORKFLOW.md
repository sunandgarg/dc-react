# Editorial and news workflow

## Publication gate

An article may be published only when it has a unique topic, useful title and description, visible structured sections, a named author, a valid date and a relevant featured image. The AI Studio quality and duplicate gates apply before persistence. Manual publishing may override topic duplication intentionally, but must not bypass factual review.

## Freshness

- Keep `created_at` as the original publication time.
- Change `updated_at` only after a material editorial update.
- Do not republish unchanged articles to simulate freshness.
- Fresh published articles enter the two-day News sitemap automatically.

## Trust and media

- Verify time-sensitive facts against official institutions before publication.
- Keep source research in the editorial workflow; do not paste reference lists into public copy unless editorially useful.
- Use a relevant 1200px or larger landscape image where possible.
- Keep image alt text descriptive and specific to the article.
- Add a YouTube URL only when the visible page genuinely embeds or links that video.

## Distribution

Publishing or materially changing an article updates normal sitemap membership, the News feed, internal recommendations and IndexNow. Search engines decide whether to crawl, index, rank or show the page in News or Discover.
